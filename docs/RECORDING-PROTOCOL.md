# Golden set recording protocol

For the founder's CC0 reference recordings. 48 kHz / 24-bit mono. Record every item twice: once on the laptop mic, once on the USB or interface mic; once in a quiet room and once with a fan or HVAC running. Keep a metronome click on a separate track for the timed items. Budget about 20 minutes of audio and a few hours of annotation.

1. **Range calibration.** Slide from your lowest comfortable note to your highest, and back, on "ah" and closed-mouth.
2. **Sustained tones.** Five seconds each on /a e i o u/ and closed-mouth hum, at low, middle and high pitch. Include one with deliberate vibrato and one straight.
3. **Phrases.** Four short phrases, each performed hummed, sung on "ah", and scatted ("da", "doo", "ba"). Include same-pitch re-articulation ("da-da-da" on one note) and at least one scoop into a note.
4. **Beatbox hits, isolated.** Twenty each of kick, snare, closed hat, open hat, plus ten each of any other sound you use (rim, clap, "pf", "ts" variants). Leave half a second between hits.
5. **Beatbox grooves.** Four bars each at 90 and 120 BPM against the click: one straight, one swung, one with fills, one "embarrassing" (as loose and expressive as you actually perform).
6. **Noise and breath.** One breathy phrase, one whispered phrase, three whooshes/risers of different lengths, some hiss and fricative textures.

Annotation: onsets of items 4 and 5 against the click (correct by hand in Reaper or Sonic Visualiser); f0 of items 2 and 3 corrected in Tony or Sonic Visualiser; hit labels for item 4 from the file names. Store under `research/data/golden/` (gitignored); publish later as a Zenodo release with a CC0 statement.
